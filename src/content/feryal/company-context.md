# Venture Foods W.L.L. (Kuwait) — Strategic Context for Feryal (Executive Director)

> **Owner-Editable Qualitative Context:** This file provides strategic business context, operational priorities, seasonal rhythms, and platform concerns for Venture Foods W.L.L. (Kuwait). It intentionally contains **no business figures, financial metrics, order counts, or targets** — all numbers must come strictly from live Odoo 17 data.

---

## 1. Company Strategic Goals
- **Protect Net Margin (حماية صافي الهامش):** Prioritize profitable sales growth rather than chasing gross revenue with deep discounts, unmonitored promotions, or high-commission channel mix drift.
- **Control Food Cost & Delivery Commission Drag (ضبط تكلفة الغذاء وعمولات التوصيل):** Monitor central kitchen procurement, recipe/portion discipline, stock scrap, and aggregator commission drag across delivery platforms.
- **Maximize Peak-Hour Throughput & Branch Discipline (تعظيم كفاءة ساعات الذروة وانضباط الفروع):** Ensure branches are staffed and stocked for high-demand dining and delivery windows while avoiding idle labor during quiet hours.
- **Data & Accounting Discipline:** Maintain clean POS register attribution (avoid unattributed buckets), reconcile aggregator payouts against ledger clearing accounts, and respect closed-month GL boundaries.

## 2. Kuwait Seasonal & Operational Calendar
- **Ramadan (رمضان):** Customer demand shifts dramatically away from daytime dining toward Futoor (sunset) surges, late-night dining, and Suhoor delivery orders. Staffing, kitchen prep, and delivery readiness must shift later into the evening and overnight hours.
- **Eid Al-Fitr & Eid Al-Adha (عيد الفطر وعيد الأضحى):** Sharp demand spikes driven by family gatherings, group/sharing meals, and heavy mall footfall (360 Mall, Avenues, Assima, Al-Kout, Promenade). Pre-event central kitchen stock build-up and branch shift coverage are critical.
- **Back-to-School (العودة للمدارس — around 14–15 Sep):** School openings around 14–15 Sep immediately boost school canteens activity as well as weekday family meal demand and morning/afternoon order patterns.
- **Payday & Weekend Cycles (دورات الرواتب والويكند):** Payday windows and Thursday–Saturday weekends drive higher ticket sizes and dine-in/mall traffic; mid-month and quiet afternoon hours require tighter cost and promo discipline.
- **Exhibition Booths (بوثات المعارض):** Event-driven seasonal sales tracked as their own company group; evaluate booth activity against event schedules and staffing costs.

## 3. Delivery Platforms & Talabat TGO vs. TMP Concerns
- **Talabat TGO vs. TMP Mix (`talabat-tgo` vs. `talabat-tmp`):**
  - **Talabat TGO (أسطول المنصة):** Uses Talabat's fleet and typically carries a higher platform commission fee structure. Heavy reliance on TGO squeezes contribution margin even when top-line sales look strong.
  - **Talabat TMP (سائقي الشركة):** Uses Venture Foods' own drivers/logistics; monitor driver utilization, dispatch times, and own-fleet operating costs versus TGO fee savings.
- **Multi-Platform Dependency & Reconciliation (تعدد المنصات والمطابقة):**
  - Across Talabat, Keeta, Deliveroo, Jahez, and Snoonu, track channel share shifts, payout timing, campaign/discount ROI, and clearing reconciliation in Odoo accounting.
  - Guard against direct dine-in and takeaway-direct customers migrating onto high-commission aggregator channels without incremental volume.

## 4. Kitchen & Branch Overtime Concerns
- **Overtime Control (ضبط الساعات الإضافية):** Avoid unnecessary overtime across branches and the Ardiya central kitchen by aligning shift schedules strictly to actual hourly POS order curves (shifting hours out of low-volume quiet hours into peak windows).
- **Central Kitchen (Ardiya) Prep & Transfers:** Balance prep batches in Ardiya against branch transfer demand and scrap rates so kitchens are neither under-prepped during peak rush nor over-producing perishable items that end in scrap or forced overtime.
- **Branch Execution Discipline:** Keep POS register mapping accurate, monitor weak-performing hours per location, and enforce manager accountability on shift handovers and stock counts.
